About Marceen

Marceen A. Burgher, MBA, MHRM is a strategic thought leader and founder building AI-enabled talent intelligence systems designed to hold under pressure. Her work integrates behavioral science, human-centered data, and operational governance to reduce ambiguity and strengthen decision integrity across organizations.

As Founder & CEO of SkillVantage Enterprise, she is developing an AI product ecosystem rooted in Talent Intelligence—advancing SkillVantage’s mission to disrupt traditional HR models. This ecosystem includes an AI-powered Talent Database, Social Network Intelligence, a Coaching Platform, and a Learning Management System (LMS), serving as a blueprint for the next generation of workforce transformation. Complementing this work is her development of an AI-Powered Emergency Management Preparedness Scorecard, a dashboard designed to improve workforce readiness, compliance visibility, and operational resilience.

Her current research includes THE FUTURE OF TALENT INTELLIGENCE: A SkillVantage White Paper: How AI, Behavioral Science, and Human-Centered Data Will Redefine Workforces by 2030 and Beyond. She also advises small businesses and hybrid initiatives on execution-ready operational and business plans, aligning strategy, delivery, and capital readiness. Her IP roadmap includes patent-supported innovations such as SkillVantage’s Talent Intelligence AI, Cenna™, and related automation frameworks.

Marceen’s leadership and design approach are reinforced by her Working Genius in Discernment and Galvanizing, alongside CliftonStrengths in Achiever, Learner, Strategic, Responsibility, and Command—reflecting her role as a driving force behind intelligent talent strategy and applied automation.
